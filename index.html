How It Works

Check in online

Complete your airline's online check-in and have your mobile boarding pass ready.

Drop off luggage

Check in your luggage at the hotel concierge or conference venue 7am-11am (for flights departing 5pm-7am the next day)

Track your luggage

Receive real-time updates as your luggage are sealed and securely transported to the airport.

Enjoy Singapore

Explore the city hands-free while your luggage is checked through to your flight.

Breeze through the airport

Head straight to immigration with your mobile boarding pass. Your luggage will meet you at your destination.
